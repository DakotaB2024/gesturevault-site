Account recovery

If you forget your gesture and saved your recovery phrase, use the Account Recovery option in GestureVault. Keep your recovery phrase somewhere safe outside the app.

Before resetting your vault

Resetting GestureVault permanently deletes the enrolled gesture, any decoy gesture and the information stored in the vaults. Use the reset option only when you intend to erase the vault and start again.
